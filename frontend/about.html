About Memoir

Memoir is your private, premium space to document your thoughts, feelings, and experiences.

It provides a simple, beautiful, and secure platform for self-expression, mindfulness, and reflection.

Features:

  • Write daily entries with ease and select your mood.
  • Automatically capture precise dates and times.
  • Easily search and filter your past entries.
  • Browse entries on an interactive monthly Calendar view.
  • Light and Dark mode toggling with saved preferences.
  • 100% private: all your data remains securely stored inside your browser.
